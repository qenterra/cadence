# Feature Overview

Cadence 1.0.2 combines a managed local music
library, native playback, organization, and lyrics in one macOS application. It does not supply a
streaming catalog, and importing music leaves the selected source files intact.

## Library and Home

- Review audio files, folders, duplicates, and matching LRC files before import.
- Copy confirmed media into the managed Cadence folder you choose.
- Browse tracks, albums, artists, playlists, tags, and smart collections.
- Find favorite tracks, albums, and artists in separate Home sections.
- Revisit Recently Played, including tracks that are also favorites.
- Move managed items to recoverable Cadence Trash and restore them later.

## Playback and lyrics

- Play managed music or open an audio file from Finder as a temporary queue.
- Control playback from the player bar, Now Playing, media keys, and Control Center.
- Inspect the current audio format, backend, and output route in Audio Path.
- Follow line-timed lyrics, seek by selecting a timed line, and edit LRC timestamps.
- Use Cadence Mode for an artwork-colored background and keyboard effects.

## Organization and editing

Hierarchical tags, rule-based smart collections, and ordered playlists serve
different organization needs. Artwork and lyrics can be edited on managed
records without rewriting the original imported files.

## Interface

System, Light, and Dark appearances share native table selection, configurable
track columns, and labeled controls. Cadence includes first-run guidance,
replayable Help chapters, and reduced-motion behavior. Hardware-specific audio,
VoiceOver, and motion acceptance remain separate from automated tests.
